Orbit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Orbit does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The game runs entirely on your device. No account or sign-up exists.
  • Scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in to Game Center, leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Orbit never sees or stores your Game Center identity beyond submitting your score.
  • Orbit contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Orbit makes no network requests of its own.

If a future version adds advertising, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before release.
