Orbit: Planet Merge — Support

Orbit is a one-thumb planet-merging puzzle game for iPhone. Drop planets, merge them from Moon to Sun, and survive the black hole.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: moha.by@icloud.com

FAQ

How does the Daily Challenge work?
Everyone in the world gets the same sequence of planets each day (UTC). Your first run counts as your official attempt; replays are practice.

Do I need an account?
No. Orbit works fully offline. Game Center sign-in is optional, for leaderboards only.

My progress disappeared.
Scores are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes its scores.

Privacy

Orbit collects no data. Read the full privacy policy.
